About The Webinar

The Paradigm Shift: From Reactive Repair to Predictive Autonomy

Global manufacturing is experiencing its steepest technological evolution since the advent of industrial automation. Traditional factories rely on retrospective telemetry and manual inspection protocols—costing industrial operators over $50 billion annually in avoidable equipment failure and scrap rework.

Hosted by MANVIAN, this intensive masterclass bridges the gap between theoretical AI models and shop-floor reality. We deconstruct real-world enterprise architectures that connect raw PLC/SCADA vibration feeds, edge-accelerated computer vision, and digital twin simulators into unified smart production lines.

  • Enterprise Edge Architecture Deploy low-latency inferencing directly at industrial machine cells without cloud bottlenecks.
  • Zero-Defect Optical Inspection Train high-accuracy convolutional vision transformers on limited defect sample sets.
  • Pragmatic 90-Day Implementation Roadmap Step-by-step framework to pilot high-yield AI initiatives with quantifiable OEE impact.
